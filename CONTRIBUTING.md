# Contributing

The Almanac accepts four types of contributions:

1. New term suggestions
2. Prompt macro suggestions
3. Source suggestions
4. Corrections or clarifications

## Editorial Rule

Use newsletters and explainers for vocabulary discovery. Use official docs, research papers, and standards for canonical definitions.

## Entry Quality Bar

A good entry should include:

- Definition
- Core concept
- Why it matters
- Real-world example
- Common mistakes
- Related terms
- Production notes
- Source notes

## Status Labels

- `seed` — captured idea, not polished
- `draft` — structured but not fully sourced
- `reviewed` — edited for clarity
- `verified` — checked against authoritative sources
- `canonical` — polished, sourced, and stable
- `deprecated` — no longer recommended or replaced by newer terminology

## Copyright Rules

Do not copy long passages from newsletters, docs, books, or articles. Paraphrase, cite, and preserve original source links.
