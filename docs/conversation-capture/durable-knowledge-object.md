# Durable Knowledge Object

## Definition

A reusable unit of knowledge extracted from a conversation or research batch.

## Examples

- Canonical term
- Prompt macro
- Decision
- Template
- Artifact
- Source note
- Open question
- Follow-up wiki entry

## Why It Matters

Summaries lose information. Durable knowledge objects preserve the pieces that can become documentation, code, checklists, or future wiki pages.
